## Gotchas (read before debugging)

* **SQLite ISO comparison**: never use `datetime('now', '-Nm')` against
  ISO-T timestamps. See `ERRORS_AND_FIXES.md` entry #4.
* **Bind-mount staleness**: after `Edit` rewrites a host file, force
  recreate the container. Entry #5.
* **Test mocks**: every new `TradingConfig` field requires updating
  `_make_cfg` in `tests/test_trader.py`. Entry #10.
* **ChromaDB telemetry warnings**: ignore. Entry #6.
* **`MULTI_CORE_ENABLE=1`**: don't enable. Entry #7.
* **`reset_cycle.py` runs against the container's `/data/seulgi.db`**;
  to run from host, stop the container first OR override `DB_PATH`
  AFTER `exec_module`.
